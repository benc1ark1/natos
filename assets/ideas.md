## Decided so far
- The build follows BUILD-GUIDE.md; my-spec.md is the brief.
- Main areas: Study, Athletic, Personal. Subjects are tags, not categories.
- Look: based on Miles Deutscher's dashboard (assets/screenshots), refined with design passes.
- Phone: dashboard added to home screen + Telegram bot opened from the iPhone Action Button, for capture, questions, morning brief and reminders.
- Google Calendar is the main schedule view and notification system ("Life OS" calendar, two-way).
- Notion Life Mastery guides become a Guides library; Morning/Night guides become daily routine checklists that never go overdue.
- Not building for now: finance, nutrition tracking.
- Simplify (4 Oct 2026): see my-spec section 42. Due/Nice, no recurring tasks, tasks never count toward targets, simple Goals.
- Tests (4 Oct 2026): a test is a goal marked Test with 2–5 rule-based prep sessions; only the next one shows on Today. Leftovers archive the day after.
- Projects + Someday (4 Oct 2026): projects stay in the background (Projects page shows the next action); Someday is the backlog and never feeds Today.
- "You forgot this" nudges (6 Oct 2026, Step 9): Telegram lists open tasks that are overdue, due today or due tomorrow (Someday never counts). They come in the 07:00 morning brief, plus one 18:00 nudge only if any are still open. Rule-based, no AI, sent at most once a day each.
